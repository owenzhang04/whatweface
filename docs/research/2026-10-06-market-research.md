# Market research: is What We Face worth building, and worth a domain?

## Answer

- **Benefit to people: real but narrow.** Demand for "understand the world's biggest problems" is proven. Our World in Data (OWID) reports roughly 100M users a year. But OWID, 80,000 Hours, Gapminder and Worldometer already serve that demand. The gap What We Face can fill is a **short, plain-language, action-first digest across three scales** (individual, humanity, earth) that makes the reader *feel* the size of each problem. That is a curation and design layer on top of existing data, not new knowledge. The benefit depends almost entirely on how good the "what you can do" sections are.
- **Traffic: expect very little unless you distribute it yourself.** Generic queries ("world's biggest problems") are increasingly answered by AI summaries in search results. A new site with no backlinks should expect near-zero organic search traffic in its first 6–12 months. That is a judgment, not a measured figure. Traffic will come from sharing (Reddit, Hacker News, teachers, social), or it won't come.
- **Money ROI: none.** There's no revenue model, and ads would undermine the credibility the citation standard is built to earn. The return is a shipped, public, well-engineered portfolio piece (connects to T008, the resume) plus whatever good the action items do.
- **Domain: worth it, but only at launch.** About $8.50 for the first year of `.org`. Don't buy `.com` as a defensive registration. See the Domains section.

## Competitor landscape

| Site | What it does | Traffic signal | Gap What We Face could fill |
|---|---|---|---|
| Our World in Data | Research and data "to make progress against the world's largest problems"; 13,000+ charts | Semrush estimate 2.18M visits (Oct 2025); OWID self-reports ~100M users/year, 89M unique visitors in 2021 | Dense and neutral by design. No emotional weight, few direct calls to action |
| 80,000 Hours problem profiles | Ranks pressing problems by scale, neglectedness, tractability (AI, pandemics, nuclear, great power war, factory farming...) | n/a | Career-focused, effective-altruism framing, long-form. Not for general public at a glance |
| Gapminder | Fact-based worldview, Hans Rosling legacy | Semrush: 164k–555k visits/month (Feb–Apr 2025) | Focuses on correcting misconceptions (often "things are better than you think"), not on calls to action |
| Worldometer | Live counters (population, deaths, CO2...) | Semrush/Ahrefs: ~3.9–4.9M visits/month (2025) | Counters without context or sourcing per number; no actions |
| Books (Gordon Brown *Seven Ways to Change the World*, Rischard *High Noon*) | Curated lists of global problems | n/a | Static, no individual or earth scale split |

Note on traffic numbers: third-party estimators (Semrush, Ahrefs) undercount large sites badly. Semrush's 2.18M/month for OWID vs OWID's own ~100M/year (≈8M/month) is a 4x gap. Use them for relative size only.

## Search and distribution environment (2025–2026)

- Median publisher traffic fell 10% year over year in H1 2025; non-news content sites fell 14% (Digital Bloom report).
- Google AI Overviews appeared on 13.14% of queries by late 2025 (up from 6.49% in Jan 2025). CTR with an AI Overview present is ~8% vs ~15% without.
- Implication: informational sites now win on **links people share** and **pages that are worth visiting rather than summarizing** (strong visuals, the "weight" moment, interactive data). That supports the design-heavy direction, but it does not guarantee traffic.

## Brand check

No existing site or organization named "What We Face" turned up in search (2026-10-06). Similar phrasing appears in book subtitles ("...the most pressing problems we face"), with no conflict.

## Domains

WHOIS on 2026-10-06: `whatweface.org`, `whatweface.com`, `whatweface.earth` all unregistered. Cloudflare Registrar sells at cost:

| TLD | Register | Renew |
|---|---|---|
| .org | $8.50 | $11.20 |
| .com | $10.46 | $10.46 |

Pricing via domainoffer.net (May 2026 snapshot), so confirm in the Cloudflare dashboard before buying. `.earth` pricing was not checked.

Recommendation:
1. Build and preview on the free `*.pages.dev` subdomain. It costs $0 and works the same.
2. Buy `whatweface.org` in Phase 4 (launch), when there is something to point it at.
3. Skip `.com` unless the site gets real traffic. The risk of someone taking an unknown 3-word name is low, and $10/year of insurance for a site with no audience is not needed.

## Distribution ideas (unvalidated)

- Being a well-cited secondary source that journalists and students can link to.

These are hypotheses. Measure with Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookieless, free) after launch.
